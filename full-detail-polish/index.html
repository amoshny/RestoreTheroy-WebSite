FULL DETAIL · SAN DIEGO

What spray wax does for your car

Full Detail includes a basic spray wax finish after the exterior hand wash. It gives the clean paint a finished shine and a layer of protection. I do not offer machine paint polishing or ceramic coating.

See Full DetailCall Ace

What is included

I hand wash the exterior, clean the wheels and wheel wells, finish the tires and apply basic spray wax. The package also includes the Interior Detail refresh. Starting price for a standard sedan is $239; I confirm your final quote before work begins.

What is different

Spray wax is a finishing step. It does not remove scratches, correct paint defects or provide the durability of a ceramic coating. If your paint has damage or heavy contamination, send photos and I will tell you what I can realistically do.
