Wash, wax and polish: what each does

Hand wash
Removes loose surface dirt and road grime.
Wax or sealant
Adds a layer of protection over clean paint. A wax or sealant won’t correct scratches.
Machine polish
Can improve gloss and make some light swirls or shallow scratches less visible. Deep scratches or damaged paint may still show.

If you mainly want a wash and an interior refresh, Essential Detail starts at $209. If you’re concerned about a mark in the paint, send a close photo in good light. I’ll explain what a one-step polish can reasonably improve after checking the paint in person.
