Which package fits your car?

For dust and loose debris, a vacuum and wipe-down may be enough. When dirt is worked into fabric, extraction is part of Deep Clean & Protect.

Interior Only Detail

From $139

For everyday dust and crumbs when only the cabin needs attention.

  • Vacuum seats, carpets and trunk
  • Clean plastics, armrests, storage pockets and jambs
  • Clean interior glass and mirrors, and apply UV protectant
  • Vacuum and surface cleaning only. No deep extraction.

Essential Detail

From $179

For regular upkeep inside and out, when the cabin needs a refresh and the exterior needs a wash.

  • Includes the Interior Only cleaning
  • Foam hand wash, wheel and wheel-well cleaning, and tire shine
  • Basic spray wax for the exterior
  • No deep extraction

Deep Clean & Protect

From $229

For family cars and daily drivers with dirt worked into the carpet or upholstery.

  • Includes Essential Detail and adds carpet and upholstery extraction
  • Standard stain, grime and pet hair treatment
  • Paint decontamination and upgraded sealant

Interior detailing questions

Will every stain or odor come out?
It depends on the material, the age and type of stain or odor, and what has already been tried. I’ll check the area and explain what’s realistic. Some marks or odors may remain.
How long does an interior detail take?
A standard Interior or Full Detail typically takes 2.5 to 4 hours. Deep treatments or heavy staining can take longer.
Do I need to provide an outlet or stay with the car?
I bring my own water and power. You can go about your day as long as I can get into the vehicle and have access to the keys if needed.

Tell me what needs cleaning

A photo of the area, your vehicle model and your ZIP code help me recommend the right package.
