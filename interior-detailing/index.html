Interior detailing questions

What is included in the $179 starting price?
Interior Detail covers vacuuming, interior surfaces, jambs, glass and UV protection. It does not include extensive extraction or heavy stain restoration. Vehicle-size adjustments and condition charges are explained above.
Which option includes exterior cleaning?
Full Detail adds the exterior hand wash, wheel and tire care, and basic spray wax. Extraction is assessed separately.
Will every stain or odor come out?
It depends on the material, the age and type of stain or odor, and what has already been tried. I’ll check the area and explain what’s realistic. Some marks or odors may remain.
How long does an interior detail take?
Interior Detail typically takes about 2 hours. Deep treatments or heavy staining can take longer.
Do I need to provide an outlet or stay with the car?
I bring my own water and power. You can go about your day as long as I can get into the vehicle and have access to the keys if needed.

Vacuuming or deep interior cleaning?

A refresh tackles loose dirt, dusty surfaces and glass. Extraction works on fabric and carpet that need more attention; it is not included in Interior Detail. If you are unsure which level fits, send close-up photos of the floor, seats and problem areas.
