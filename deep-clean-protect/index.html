Questions about this detail

Is this a deep interior cleaning service?
Yes. Depending on the vehicle, I can quote carpet or upholstery extraction and focused interior cleaning. Exterior care can be added if requested. I confirm the work and price before starting.
How long does a deep clean take?
Time depends on the vehicle and the treatment agreed with Ace.
Are stain and odor removal guaranteed?
No. Results depend on the material, age and cause of the problem, and previous treatment. Some stains or odors may remain. Send photos and describe the problem before choosing a service.
What about heavy stains or pet hair?
Light spot treatment is included in the two everyday packages where practical. Deep cleaning and heavy buildup are quoted separately. I’ll agree any extra work with you before starting. Some stains may remain.
Do I need to provide water or power?
No. I bring both. I just need an accessible place to park and work.
