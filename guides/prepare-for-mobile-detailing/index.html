BEFORE YOUR MOBILE APPOINTMENT

How to prepare for mobile detailing

I bring the detailing equipment, water and power. A little planning around access, parking and the vehicle’s condition helps us arrange a practical visit.

Check the area and parking access

Use the service-area list before requesting a visit. Tell me where the car will be parked and about any entry instructions. For apartment or workplace parking, check that the property permits detailing and that there is space to work around the car.

You don’t need to supply a hose or outlet

My mobile setup brings its own water and power. You still need to provide access to the vehicle, and keys if needed. I’ll discuss the working location with you when confirming the visit.

Share the areas that need attention

Send photos of seats, floors, the cargo area or exterior buildup. Mention excessive pet hair, persistent odors and any cleaning you have already tried. This helps me explain suitable treatment and realistic results before work begins.

Plan for the selected service

The approximate service times are about 2 hours for Interior Detail, about 3 hours for Full Detail; deep cleaning time is quoted after an assessment. Size, condition and additional treatment can change the time required. You do not need to stay for the entire detail as long as access is arranged.

Confirm the quote and appointment with me

Submitting the form sends a request; it does not automatically reserve a confirmed slot. I’ll review the vehicle information and agree on the service, time and any extra work with you. If inspection is needed, I’ll confirm the final quote before starting.

Do I need to choose a package before asking?
You can compare the package guide or text photos to discuss your needs. I can help distinguish a maintenance refresh from deeper fabric cleaning.
Can you work anywhere in a listed neighborhood?
The area list indicates service locations, not permission to work on every property. Parking and access still need to be confirmed.

Not sure what your car needs?

Request a detail online or text photos of the areas that need attention. I’ll discuss the work and confirm your quote and appointment.
