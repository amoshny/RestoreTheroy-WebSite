A PRACTICAL DETAILING GUIDE

Which detailing package does your car need?

Start with what needs cleaning: a cabin refresh, regular care inside and out, or dirt worked into fabric. Here’s how Restore Theory’s three core packages differ.

Interior Only: the cabin needs a refresh

From $169 for a standard sedan, approximately 1.5 hours. Choose it for loose crumbs, dust, interior surfaces and glass when exterior care is not your priority. Vacuuming and surface cleaning are included; extensive extraction and heavy stain restoration are not.

Read the full Interior Only inclusions

Essential Detail: regular care inside and out

From $209, approximately 2.5 hours. This adds a foam hand wash, wheel and tire care, and basic spray wax to the interior refresh. It suits regularly maintained vehicles that need both the cabin and exterior addressed. Deep fabric extraction is not included.

Explore Essential Detail

Deep Clean & Protect: more than a surface clean

From $269, approximately 3.5 hours. This adds carpet and upholstery extraction, standard stain, grime and pet-hair treatment, chemical paint decontamination and upgraded sealant. It is the deeper option for family cars and daily drivers needing more attention.

See Deep Clean & Protect details

What changes the starting estimate?

Starting prices are for standard sedans. Mid-size SUVs add $25; large SUVs and minivans add $50. RVs are 2× the base price. Compact SUVs and pickups need a quote. Heavy buildup or excessive pet hair may require additional treatment agreed before work begins. See the current Extras & Add-ons.

Show the condition, not just the vehicle model

A close photo of a dirty floor or seat is more useful than a distant photo of the whole car. Include the area you want treated and mention odors or previous cleaning attempts. Stains and odors vary; no package guarantees every mark will disappear.
