FAQ

Do you need access to water and power?
Yes. Since I operate a fully mobile setup, I require access to a standard electrical outlet and a water spigot at your location. If you live in an apartment complex without access, please let me know so we can discuss options.
Do I need to be present for the whole detail?
Not at all! As long as the vehicle is unlocked, keys are available if needed, and I have access to power and water, you can leave me to work while you go about your day.
How long does a detail take?
A standard Interior or Full Detail typically takes 2.5 to 4 hours. Deep interior treatments or heavy stain removals may take longer. I focus on quality over speed.
Can you remove all stains and odors?
I use professional extraction and treatments that eliminate the vast majority of stains and odors. However, some severe set-in stains may be permanently etched into the fabric. I will always set realistic expectations before starting.
How do I pay?
I accept Cash, Zelle, Venmo, and major Credit Cards. Payment is due upon completion and your final inspection of the vehicle.
