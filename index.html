FAQ

Do you come to me?
Yes. Restore Theory is a 100% mobile service. I bring all the necessary equipment directly to your home, apartment complex, or workplace.
How long does a detail take?
A standard Interior or Full Detail typically takes 2.5 to 4 hours. Deep interior treatments or heavy stain removals may take longer. I focus on quality over speed.
Do I need to provide water or electricity?
In most cases, having access to an electrical outlet and a water spigot is helpful, especially at residential homes. If you live in an apartment without access, let me know in advance so we can plan accordingly.
Can you remove all stains and odors?
I use professional extraction and treatments that eliminate the vast majority of stains and odors (including pet accidents and smoke). However, some severe set-in stains may be permanently etched into the fabric. I will always set realistic expectations before starting.
How do I pay?
I accept Cash, Zelle, Venmo, and major Credit Cards. Payment is due upon completion and your final inspection of the vehicle.
